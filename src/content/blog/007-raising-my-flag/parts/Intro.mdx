_Clair Obscur: Expedition 33_ is one of the best games I have ever played.

I'm not really a fan of turn based games. It was my first one, and I only tried it because friends kept talking me into it. It was
just awesome. It was quite hard too, which made me love it even more, and artistically it's a masterpiece, the VFX especially.

It's not my all time favourite, but I still wanted to pay it a small homage somewhere in [Revo](https://revo-realms.aleksandargjoreski.dev/).

The flag felt like the perfect match. It's a symbol for the expedition, and it's also something I had wanted to learn for a while:
cloth simulation.

<br />

I never considered animating it in Blender. First, because I have no idea how to animate cloth in Blender (I would have learned, to
be fair). Second, and more importantly, because I wanted it to be interactive. That was the whole point. I wanted the flag to react
to the wind and to the ball rolling into it, not to play the same loop forever.

I was already familiar with compute shaders from the [grass](/blog/trimming-my-grass-shader/), so this felt like a good excuse to
learn how cloth is actually simulated and what techniques are behind it.
