## Closing thoughts

Of all the articles I published so far, this one was by far the hardest to write, not even close.

Building the shadows was a long journey already, but writing about them turned into a rabbit hole of its own. Every time I went back to
the code to get a recording or a screenshot right, I'd find something else that felt like it had to be mentioned, rewrite a
paragraph, and then start doubting the one before it. At some point I was questioning pretty much every choice in here.

Somewhere along the way I think I may have realised why. Every detail felt important because I remembered what each one had cost
me to figure out.

A sphere casting a cube shaped shadow, a flat plane quietly throwing the whole cache away, chasing a bit of lag on a laptop that
wasn't even my main one. To me each of those deserved a section of its own, while a reader mostly needs a sentence, sometimes not
even that.

Every one of them was part of the journey, but telling all of them would have turned this into a book with a four hour reading
time, and this was meant to be an article. So some of them made it in, and some got cut.

It gave me a whole new respect for the people who can take something this tangled, explain it in plain words, stay concise where it
matters and know what to leave out.

That's a skill of its own, and clearly I still have a long way to go :)
