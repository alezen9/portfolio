## Wrapping Up

I'm not completely sold on this water yet, but I'm very happy with the direction it's taking. More importantly, I now
have enough controls to experiment with different moods without rewriting the shader itself.

There are, of course, more things that could push it further: screen-space reflections, dynamic ripples that react
to movement, buoyancy to make objects float naturally. Maybe I'll explore those in the future.

For now, I'll leave it here: a shader that taught me a lot, gave me countless hours of fun, and stands as my humble
take on water in real-time graphics.
